# 📈 Interest Rate Prediction Model

## 📌 Objective
Predict interest rates assigned to financial clients based on historical data.

## 💼 Business Context
Interest rate assignment is directly linked to risk assessment and profitability. This model helps improve pricing consistency and decision-making.

## ⚙️ Approach
Two modeling strategies were implemented:
- PyCaret (automated ML)
- Scikit-learn + LightGBM (manual pipeline)

## 📊 Metrics
- RMSE
- MAE
- R²

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Higher credit exposure leads to higher interest rates
- Loan structure variables (amount, duration) are dominant drivers
- Model shows strong generalization and low bias

## 💡 Business Impact
- Supports risk-based pricing
- Improves decision consistency
- Enables scalable rate assignment

## 🛠️ Tools
- Python (pandas, sklearn, lightgbm)
- PyCaret
- Matplotlib
